Research agenda

From data to reliable intelligence.

Three connected research directions for prospective collaborators and students.

01 / GEOAI

Trustworthy spatial AI

How can AI reason about geographic space, assess missing evidence, and safely perform complex spatial tasks? I am interested in spatial agents, uncertainty-aware reasoning, and the reliability of automated GIS workflows.

GeoAIAI agentsSpatial reasoning
02 / GRAPHS

Knowledge graphs & data fusion

How can we combine heterogeneous sources while preserving their provenance and uncertainty? We develop knowledge representations and graph-based learning methods for scientific discovery, mobility, and decision support.

Knowledge graphsGNNsData fusion
03 / PEOPLE

Human-centered AI

How can intelligent systems augment human judgment rather than replace it? We explore wearable AI, learning technologies, and responsible applications of language models in education and society.

HCIWearable AIAI for education

Research culture

Learn by building and investigating.

I mentor graduate and undergraduate researchers from computing, data science, AI, and other disciplines. I aim to help students develop independent research skills, learn how to ask meaningful questions, and communicate their findings clearly.

Students I have mentored have contributed to peer-reviewed papers, received undergraduate research fellowships, participated in NSF I-Corps, and earned recognition for master's research.

What I value in research

Independent thinking, reproducible experiments, thoughtful collaboration, and a willingness to investigate why a method works—not just whether it works.
